Snow Leopard

⛰️Snow leopards are found in the mountain ranges of Central and South Asia, including the Himalayas, at elevations of about 3,000–5,500 meters (10,000–18,000 feet).

🐱Their spotted gray fur blends perfectly with rocky, snowy landscapes, making them very difficult to spot in the wild.

💧 They don't always need to drink much water because they can get moisture from the animals they eat.

🐆❄️ Very interesting fact: A snow leopard's tail can be almost as long as its body! It uses its huge, fluffy tail like a blanket, wrapping it around its face while sleeping to keep warm in freezing mountain temperatures. 🥶🏔️
